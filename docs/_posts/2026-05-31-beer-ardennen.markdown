We recently took a roadtrip from the Netherlands to the Ardennen in Belgium. Its a beautiful green region that is situated between southeast Belgium, northern Luxembourg and France. The areas we visited were in Belgian region of Wallonia namely the cities of Dinant, La Roche-en-Ardennen and Durbuy. The scenery, weather and history was pleasant. I quickly discovered that there was no shortage of new beers to try, an activity which I found most enjoyable.

Some fun facts:
* After I learnt that both the inventor of the Saxaphone (Aldolph Sax) + and the Belgian surrealist artist, René Magritte (Ceci n'est pas une pipe), originated from Belgium. I finally understood the meaning of Ceci n'est pas un Saxophone.
* The Maneblusser beer literally means extinguishing the moon, the host that served us food in Mechelen told us the urban legend that a drunk man, thinking a Cathedral was on fire, alerted the townsfolk and tried to extinguish the "fire". Only to later realise it was the bright light of the moon shining through the mist.
