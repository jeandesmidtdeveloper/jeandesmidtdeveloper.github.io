# Rating system
- Taste (10) - How good is the texture, quality taste of the ingredients
- Service (10) - How is the hospitality of the waiters and staff at the establishment.
- Feel (Ambience) (10) - How does the restaurant make you feel.
- Look (Aesthetics) (10) - What does it look like inside 
- Creativity/Curation/Uniqueness/Novelty (10) - Is there anything unique  about this place that is hard to replicate at another establishment?
